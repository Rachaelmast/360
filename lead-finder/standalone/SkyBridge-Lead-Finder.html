1. Find the facility behind a Vivian job

On a Vivian job page, click the Copy for SkyBridge bookmark, or press Ctrl+U, then Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C in the source tab. Then paste below. The page is read here on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Set up the one-click “Copy for SkyBridge” bookmark

Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it: Copy for SkyBridge

It copies the open page and its URL to your clipboard when you click it, and does nothing else.

2. Saved facilities

Click a facility to edit its status, owner, follow-up, notes, or merge duplicates. To combine a teammate's leads, restore their backup file: new facilities are added and nothing is overwritten.
